My Story

Stories have always been part of my journey.

Writing became a way to give voice to experiences, emotions and ideas that deserved to be remembered.

I'm Kitsamba Joshua, a Ugandan novelist and storyteller with a passion for crafting compelling and thought-provoking fiction. I draw inspiration from everyday life, culture and the vibrant experiences of African people.

My education took me through Kireka Hill Infant School in Kireka, Ebenezer Primary School in Wakiso, Nabumali High School, and Hawthorne Scribner High School. Across those stages of life, my love for storytelling continued to grow.

I came to understand that stories are among the most powerful ways of carrying truth, emotion and meaning from one person to another.

Through my writing, I aim to inspire readers with stories rooted in resilience, hope and transformation. My novels explore love, loss, survival, destiny and the interconnectedness of human experience.

Whether I am writing about grief, unexpected love, difficult choices or the pursuit of a better future, I want every story to leave the reader with something meaningful.

Purpose behind the writing

Mission. Vision. Passion.

Kitsamba Writes Africa is not only about publishing stories. It is about creating a home where African voices, culture and human experiences can be shared with the world.

My Mission

To tell authentic African stories that celebrate our culture, inspire readers and contribute to a deeper global understanding of the African experience.

My Vision

To grow Kitsamba Writes Africa into a respected home for African literary excellence where authentic voices are celebrated and cultural heritage is honored.

My Passion

To create narratives that touch the heart, challenge the mind and elevate African storytelling for readers both on the continent and around the world.
